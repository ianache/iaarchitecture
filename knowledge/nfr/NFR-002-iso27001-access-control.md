Access to production systems, administrative functions, and sensitive data must follow the principle of least privilege.

Requirements:
- Every user and service account must have a unique identity and explicit authorization for the minimum functions required.
- Privileged access requires multi-factor authentication and a documented approval path.
- Access rights must be reviewed at regular intervals and revoked on role changes, contract termination, or suspicious activity.
- Administrative actions must be attributable to an accountable actor and auditable.

This requirement aligns with ISO/IEC 27001 controls for access control, user provisioning, and accountability.
